# Eight strings of a shared environment

VSB is eight shared Level16 lanes. It does not claim that the world consists of eight values. It is an engineering contract selecting eight projections important to a personality.

## The press

```text
raw environment -> normalization -> 8 x Level16 -> VSB tape
```

Changing lane meaning, normalization, or time boundaries gives the same `.d8p` a different world.

## One frame for all

Every ACTIVE tile receives the same VSB frame. Diversity comes from different signed weights, accumulated histories, corridors, decay, and positions in the permission graph.

The hydraulic analogy is exact here: one manifold presents eight pressures to all chambers, while each chamber's geometry determines its response.

## Whaler example

A market press can map motion, range energy, tempo, buy/sell pressure, mass, absorption, and imbalance into normalized lanes. Those meanings belong to the press, not Decima.

Tiles hear one market simultaneously, but different organs integrate different combinations. FIRE means history entered a designed state, not that one lane merely crossed a level.

Eight lanes impose discipline, but sufficiency must be demonstrated on independent history rather than assumed from the number eight.
