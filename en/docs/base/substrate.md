# The substrate executes; it does not define purpose

The baker defines personality; the substrate implements its physics. Today the verifiable reference is the software runtime. FPGA or ASIC becomes compatible only after conformance testing.

Portability requires identical `.d8p` interpretation, arithmetic, clamping, phase order, initial state, complete tape, reset schedule, event trace, and readout.

Different speed is acceptable. Different behaviour means a different runtime, not “the same personality on another body.”

The score analogy needs all roles:

- `.d8p` is the reaction score;
- the baker is composer and instrument engineer;
- the VSB tape is the performed sequence;
- runtime is the orchestra;
- trace is the recording.

“The substrate is secondary” means intent is independent of one CPU. It does not mean that FPGA, ASIC, or biology are already proven equivalent implementations.
