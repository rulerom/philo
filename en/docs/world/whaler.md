# Whaler: personality as an event filter

Whaler is the first practical case where architecture roles can be seen without metaphysics.

```text
market -> level-v3 press -> 8-lane VSB tape
       -> Decima personality -> rare pattern event
       -> context/risk director -> execution or skip
```

The market produces an irreversible trade sequence. The press maps it into normalized motion, range energy, tempo, side pressure, mass, absorption, and imbalance.

The baker constructs the hydraulic personality: branches that accumulate pressure, release it, retain lock, and emit an event.

Decima lives through the tape. Every ACTIVE tile hears one frame while different signed matrices integrate different relationships. A signal appears rarely, after several phases of history align inside a designed organ.

The director decides whether action is permitted by position, context, and risk.

Input frequency is not decision frequency. Millions of messages may produce a handful of events. Silence is often the correct filter output.

Whaler does not prove Decima is universal. It demonstrates something narrower and important: the architecture can operate as an event sensor in a real noisy environment.
